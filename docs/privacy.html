奇門羅盤 隱私權政策

生效日期：2026-11-17　開發者：LC Lab

我們不蒐集的資料

你寫下的紀錄、事由、筆記與應驗結果只存在你的手機，不會上傳。排盤、羅盤、出行訣都在手機上離線計算。真太陽時使用你自己選的城市，不讀取定位。

手機上的感測器與權限

  • 指南針（磁力計）：顯示方位，資料不離開手機。
  • 計步（活動辨識）：出行訣走路、跑步時計步，不上傳。
  • 通知：吉時推播、每日提醒、紀錄回顧提醒，可在設定關閉。

匿名使用統計與當機報告

使用 Google Firebase（Analytics、Crashlytics、Remote Config）了解哪些功能被使用、修正當機。不包含你輸入的任何文字。可在「設定 → 分享匿名使用統計與當機報告」關閉。

意見回饋

你主動送出的意見回饋會存到 Google Firebase Firestore，內容包含你寫的文字、選填的信箱、App 版本、平台與介面語言，只用於回覆與改進 App。

廣告

免費版顯示 Google AdMob 廣告。AdMob 可能使用廣告識別碼；需要同意的地區會先詢問你，之後可在「設定 → 廣告隱私設定」更改。會員不載入廣告。

訂閱

訂閱由 Google Play／App Store 處理付款，經 RevenueCat 確認會員狀態；我們拿不到你的付款資料。

兒童

本 App 不針對 13 歲以下兒童。

聯絡

請在 App 內「設定 → 意見回饋」與我們聯絡。

Qimen Compass Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-11-17. Developer: LC Lab.

Your records, notes and outcomes stay on your phone and are never uploaded. Charts, the compass and travel timing are computed offline. True solar time uses a city you pick; we never read your location.

The compass sensor, step counter and notifications are used only on your device. Notifications can be turned off in Settings.

Google Firebase (Analytics, Crashlytics, Remote Config) collects anonymous usage and crash data, never anything you type. Turn it off in Settings → Share anonymous usage stats & crash reports.

Feedback you choose to send is stored in Google Firebase Firestore with your message, optional email, app version, platform and language, and is used only to reply and improve the app.

The free version shows Google AdMob ads, which may use the advertising ID; where consent is required you are asked first and can change it in Settings → Ad privacy settings. Premium members see no ads.

Subscriptions are billed by Google Play / the App Store and verified through RevenueCat; we never receive your payment details.

The app is not directed at children under 13. Contact us via Settings → Send feedback.
